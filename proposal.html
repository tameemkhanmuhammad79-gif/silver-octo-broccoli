The Bottleneck

Weak lists stall outreach before it starts

Poor-fit accounts, outdated details, and manual research burn hours before the first message ever goes out.

01. Poor-Fit Accounts

Companies outside your niche, size, or service tier that cannot buy from you.

02. Outdated Details

Departed leaders, non-existent titles, and inactive shared mailboxes.

03. Manual Searching

Hours wasted clicking through directories before any contact record is usable.

04. Lost Focus

Founder and team bandwidth pulled away from client delivery and revenue work.

The Method

A better way to build a list

Every list starts from the criteria you agree on — not from a directory search. Precision first, volume second. Your ICP is the contract. Every record must earn its place.

  • 01. Agree the ICP: Niche, geography, company profile, and target roles.
  • 02. Assess each firm: Each candidate is manually checked against agreed criteria.
  • 03. Organize for outreach: Records are structured for direct action with live source links.
Honest Limits
